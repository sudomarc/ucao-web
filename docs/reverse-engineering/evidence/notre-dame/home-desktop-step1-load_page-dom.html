Health and well-being

From preventing certain UTIs to developing mental health interventions, Notre Dame research and scholarship is leading to a healthier society.

Where challenge meets hope.

Notre Dame scholars and researchers pursue breakthroughs for a world in need.

United by our commitment to the common good, the global Notre Dame community builds bridges between the world we know and the world we desire. What would you fight for? For us, it is not just a question. It's a call to action.

  • Providing second chances

    Through Notre Dame’s Inspired Leadership Initiative, Marty Whalen turned a second act into second chances for formerly incarcerated people.

  • Using AI to prevent online abuse

    With athletes noting increases in cyberstalking and harassment, Notre Dame is leveraging the power of AI to identify and stop abuse.

  • Prevent infections

    Notre Dame researchers have created a novel catheter adaptation that prevents infections before they begin.

  • Access to clean water

    More than 700 million people lack access to clean water, but research from the University of Notre Dame is helping organizations optimize how they manage, maintain, and build water pumps to reduce water scarcity.

  • Robotic search and rescue

    Notre Dame engineer develops vine robot to help first responders after disasters.

  • Nonverbal communication

    Notre Dame researchers are developing AI technology designed to help caregivers interpret the nonverbal communication of people with profound intellectual and multiple disabilities.

Addressing pressing challenges

Discovery at Notre Dame is grounded in ethical inquiry and a commitment to human flourishing. Across disciplines, our faculty and students are leading where they are needed most, working with partners to bring research and scholarship to bear on urgent challenges at home and abroad.
