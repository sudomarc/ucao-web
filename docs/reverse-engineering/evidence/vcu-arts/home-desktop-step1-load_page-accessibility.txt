    - heading "The Art Foundation Program Building a strong first-year experience" [level=3]
    - paragraph: The Art Foundation Program (known as AFO) is a one-year immersive program for all visual arts and design majors. This intellectually rigorous, studio-based experience gives you a comprehensive grounding in the fundamental issues of art and design. New materials, strategies and ideas will challenge you while expanding your skills and helping you set your direction at VCUarts.
